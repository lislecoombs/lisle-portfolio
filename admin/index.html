hello, I'm
Lisle 🌼

I make things. Paintings, drawings, sculptures, photographs — anything that helps me pay attention to the world a bit more carefully.

My work tends to start with something small and ordinary — a bit of bark, the way light hits a window, mud, or a pile of autumn leaves — and turns into something I didn't quite plan.

I like process as much as product. The studio is a bit chaotic, the sketchbooks are full, and there's always something drying somewhere it shouldn't be 🍂
